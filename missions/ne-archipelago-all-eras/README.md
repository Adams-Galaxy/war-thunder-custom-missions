# Nuclear Escalation — Archipelago lower eras

These are the official Nuclear Escalation era branches adapted to the same
four-mode progression as the maintained 2018 missions.

| Era | Battle-rating bracket | Strategic defenses |
|---|---:|---|
| 1970 Early | 8.7–9.7 | S-75 and early HAWK |
| 1970 Late | 10.0–11.0 | Buk and 1970 HAWK |
| 1980 | 11.3–13.0 | Buk-M1, S-300, HAWK, and Patriot |

Each era has four variants:

- **Normal:** the official era roster and behavior.
- **SEAD Training:** strategic batteries remain present and targetable but
  cannot engage. Shorter-range defenses stay stock.
- **Easy:** strategic batteries remain present but silent; directly
  configurable gun AA is capped at 4 km and Osa/Roland at 8 km.
- **Peaceful Exploration:** all map units remain present, while players are
  continuously hidden from AI targeting and made immortal.

All non-normal variants include 12 aircraft spawns, six helicopter spawns,
four naval spawns, 30-second respawns, and a 12 km missile-ship engagement
cap. Tanks remain disabled. The existing [2018 family](../ne-archipelago-2018/README.md)
keeps its original stable URLs.
