# Nuclear Escalation — Southeastern City lower eras

These are the official Nuclear Escalation era branches adapted to the same
four-mode progression as the maintained 2018 missions.

| Era | Battle-rating bracket | Strategic defenses |
|---|---:|---|
| 1970 Early | 8.7–9.7 | S-75 and early HAWK |
| 1970 Late | 10.0–11.0 | Buk and 1970 HAWK |
| 1980 | 11.3–13.0 | Buk-M1, S-300, HAWK, and Patriot |

Each era has Normal, SEAD Training, Easy, and Peaceful Exploration variants.
The behavior matches the [Archipelago lower-era family](../ne-archipelago-all-eras/README.md):
strategic sites are physically retained in every mode; only Easy receives the
4 km gun-AA and 8 km Osa/Roland reductions.

All non-normal variants include ten aircraft spawns, four helicopter spawns,
four naval spawns, 30-second respawns, and a 12 km missile-ship engagement
cap. Tanks remain disabled. The existing [2018 family](../ne-southeastern-city/README.md)
keeps its original stable URLs.
